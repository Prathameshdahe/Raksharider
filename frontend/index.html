ROADWATCH.AI

Saw dangerous driving? Send the clip. A person decides.

RoadWatch checks dashcam and phone footage for unsafe behaviour on Indian roads.
The AI reads every frame and writes a report; a trained reviewer confirms or rejects every finding.
Nothing is ever fined here — confirmed cases go to the traffic authority, who decide what happens next.

How a clip is handled

01 · YOU

Upload and describe

Pick two-wheeler or four-wheeler, say what you saw, add the plate if you read it. Your file goes straight to private storage.

02 · AI FIRST

Every frame, every vehicle

The pipeline tracks each vehicle, reads plates twice, and answers your claim: supported, not supported, or cannot tell. Serious reports go first.

03 · HUMAN ALWAYS DECIDES

A reviewer rules on each finding

Confirmed, rejected with a reason, or inconclusive. You see every decision on your submission. Nothing counts until a person says so.

Privacy. Uploaders see only the plate they typed; every other vehicle is "Vehicle #2, #3" with no plate. Evidence lives in private storage and opens only through short-lived links. Reviewer names and notes are never shown to uploaders. Rejected findings are kept as unconfirmed observations, never as penalties. RoadWatch never issues or collects a fine.
