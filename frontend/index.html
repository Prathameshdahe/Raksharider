How a clip is handled

Three steps. The person in the middle is always a person.

Serious reports go first. Accuracy beats speed: a clip can take minutes, and every finding is tied to exactly one vehicle with its own evidence frames.

01
You

Upload and describe

Pick two-wheeler or four-wheeler, say what you saw, add the plate if you read it. Your file goes straight to private storage over a one-time link.

02
AI first

Every frame, every vehicle

The pipeline tracks each vehicle, reads plates on the vehicle's own crop, and answers your claim: supported, not supported, or cannot tell.

03
A human always decides

A reviewer rules on each finding

Confirmed, rejected with a reason, or inconclusive. You see every decision on your submission. Nothing counts until a person says so.

Who does what

Three roles, one rule: the AI never decides.

Roles are assigned by an admin and checked on every request by the backend. Reviewer access is granted only after a badge check.

Citizen

Reports

Uploads clips (10 a day, 200 MB each), follows each one in plain words, sees only the plate they typed, and can withdraw before a reviewer starts.

Reviewer

Decides

Claims a case from the queue, inspects the evidence per finding, corrects the plate or vehicle type, and confirms, rejects with a reason, or marks inconclusive.

Admin

Runs the system

Watches the live board and the AI worker, pauses intake, requeues failures, approves reviewers, reopens or reassigns cases, approves escalation packages, reads the audit log.

Privacy and fairness

Built so a typo never convicts the wrong owner.

Uploaders see only the plate they typed; every other vehicle is "Vehicle #2, #3" with no plate. Evidence lives in private storage and opens only through short-lived signed links. Reviewer names and notes are never shown to uploaders.

What is kept, and what is not

  • Rejected findings stay on record as unconfirmed observations, never as penalties.
  • A finding cannot be confirmed with zero evidence frames.
  • Every reviewer and admin action is written to an append-only audit log.
  • RoadWatch never issues or collects a fine.

AI recommends. Humans decide. Authorities enforce.

The pipeline tracks each vehicle with one tracker per class group, reads plates on the vehicle's own crop so ownership is known by construction, and measures verdicts in time, not frame counts. Everything it reports is provisional until a person says otherwise.
